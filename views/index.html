🚄 About Railway Internship Portal

🎯 Our Mission

The Railway Internship Portal is a flagship initiative by Indian Railways designed to bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and practical industry experience. We provide students with hands-on exposure to cutting-edge railway technologies and operations across various engineering disciplines.
